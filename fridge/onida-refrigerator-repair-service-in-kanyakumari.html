Onida Refrigerator Parts We Check or Replace

Simple explanations of essential components tested during our doorstep service visits in Kanyakumari.

Hermetic Cooling Compressor

The primary pump that produces refrigeration. If it overheats, makes loud vibrations, or trips the home circuit breaker, it needs inspection.

PTC Starter Relay

Supplies initial starting power to the compressor windings. A clicking sound every few minutes indicates a burnt starter relay.

Direct Cool Thermostat Switch

Controls the cooling cycle in single door models. If the bellows fail, items inside either freeze completely or stay warm.

Frost-Free Blower Fan Motor

Circulates cold air through the food compartment in double door models. If stuck, the lower compartment stays warm.

Mechanical Defrost Timer

Switches the fridge into defrost mode automatically. If internal contacts wear out, frost blocks the cooling fins.

Defrost Heater Tube

Melts ice off the cooling coil so air can circulate. If broken, frost chokes the back duct within a few days.

Cabinet Door Gasket

Seals the door tightly against the cabinet frame. If cracked or hard, cold air leaks out continuously.

Drain Trough & Hose

Carries defrost meltwater away. If choked with dust or food particles, water pools inside the vegetable tray.

When Should You Call for Onida Refrigerator Repair?

Look out for these warning signs to avoid food spoilage and costly compressor replacements.

  • ❄️ Cooling becoming low: Fresh food or milk spoiling quickly on lower shelves.
  • 🧊 Freezer not freezing: Ice cubes not setting or ice cream melting inside the freezer.
  • 💧 Water leakage: Water pooling beneath the vegetable crisper or leaking on the kitchen floor.
  • 🧱 Excess frost buildup: Dense ice accumulating on the freezer back wall, choking air vents.
  • 🔊 Compressor noise or clicking: Repeated clicking sounds every 2 to 3 minutes without cooling.
  • 🚪 Door not sealing: Moisture droplets forming around door frames due to weak magnetic gaskets.
  • ⚠️ Repeated tripping or blinking: Fridge cutting power or digital display flashing error codes.
  • ⚡ Unusual electrical smell: If you smell burning insulation, switch off the appliance immediately, unplug the cable, and call for inspection.
